Melomakarona

Greek Christmas honey cookies with orange, cinnamon, and walnuts.

50 min prep 105 min baking across batches 75–80 cookies

Instructions

Allow 3–4 hours for the syrup to cool. You can make it a day ahead.

  1. Make the syrup. Bring the water, sugar, cinnamon sticks, whole cloves, and orange halves to a boil. When the sugar dissolves, take the pan off the heat and stir in the honey. Let the syrup cool completely.
  2. Mix the dough. Heat the oven to 190°C fan and line a baking tray with parchment. Whisk the juice, oils, icing sugar, ground spices, baking soda, and zest in one bowl. Mix the flour and semolina in another bowl. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir gently with a spoon for about 10 seconds.
  3. Shape and bake. Shape 30 g portions into ovals about 3–4 cm long. Roll each cookie lightly over a wire skimmer to make the pattern. Bake 16–20 cookies per tray for 20–25 minutes, until golden and crisp. Plan on 4–5 batches.
  4. Dip in cold syrup. As soon as each tray comes out of the oven, dip the hot cookies in the cold syrup for about 10 seconds. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and drain on a wire rack.
  5. Finish with honey and walnuts. Arrange the cookies on a platter, drizzle with honey, and scatter the chopped walnuts on top.
